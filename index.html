My approach

Research first. Promote responsibly.

A simple process I use when planning a marketing concept.

Research

Understand the audience, their needs, and search intent.

Match

Consider whether an offer is relevant and permitted for the channel.

Create

Prepare useful content and a clear, honest landing page.

Evaluate

Review real analytics when available; learn and improve without inventing results.
